Privacy Policy

Trader Grims Warehouse

Trader Grims Warehouse uses this site and its connected software tools to support eBay account authorization, listing management, inventory synchronization, order-related processing, and related internal business operations.

Information Used

When you authorize the application, the system may access information made available through the eBay APIs, including account information, listing information, inventory information, order-related marketplace data, and authorization credentials such as access tokens and refresh tokens.

How Information Is Used

This information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve Trader Grims Warehouse tools and workflows, including catalog management, listing creation, inventory updates, and related business processes.

Sharing

Trader Grims Warehouse does not sell eBay account data or authorization data. Information is used for internal business purposes except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to provide services specifically requested by the authorized account owner.

Access Scope

The application requests only the access reasonably necessary to perform its intended functions. Access to eBay data depends on the permissions granted during the eBay authorization process.

Security and Retention

Reasonable administrative and technical measures are used to protect credentials, tokens, and related marketplace data. Authorized data is retained only as needed for operational, recordkeeping, security, and compliance purposes.

Revoking Access

If you want to revoke access, you may do so through the relevant eBay authorization or account controls. Future application access depends on valid authorization remaining in place.

Contact

For questions regarding this policy, contact Trader Grims Warehouse through the business contact information associated with this site.
