# Clarification: one gated acceptance path for all research submissions

**Status:** retained design clarification — no implementation authorized
**Owner:** Dave; Tigwa maintains staging/provenance
**Date:** 2026-07-20
**Supersedes only in scope:** the guided-session-only boundary in `RESEARCH-perplexity-guided-research-operator-acceptance-gate-2026-07-20.md`

## Direction

The same operator-gated target save path applies to **all research submissions**, not only guided Perplexity sessions.

Covered submission forms include:

- guided-session captures and exports;
- Perplexity MCP/API search, reasoning, and Deep Research outputs;
- manual source captures and Dave-supplied research material;
- agent-created source inventories, literature/product research, and advisory syntheses;
- future third-party research-provider outputs.

## Required boundary

Every submission first enters a staged-evidence location with acquisition provenance and an integrity reference appropriate to its retained bytes. It does not automatically become canonical/research-shelf material, PP input, or implementation input.

Only explicit operator acceptance selects the named destination/category and intended role. Acceptance remains distinct from review, synthesis, decision adoption, and implementation authorization.

## Non-goals

This does not prescribe the eventual directory/database/UI implementation, authorize a save-path migration, or change any existing canonical artifact. It is the governing workflow constraint for future research-intake design.
