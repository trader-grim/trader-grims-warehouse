## Hypotheses

1. **Standalone camera application**
   - Generalize, complete, and polish the TGW camera app into a useful standalone Play Store product.
   - Potential value: useful independent utility and a TGW-adjacent acquisition/product surface.

2. **End-to-end clipboard event server**
   - Develop the explicit-payload, authenticated clipboard-event-server concept as both an open-source project and a hosted service for people who do not want to self-host.
   - Potential value: open-source adoption/trust plus managed-service recurring revenue.

3. **Complementary product pair**
   - The camera app and clipboard-event server may work together and cross-sell each other.
   - This is an ecosystem hypothesis, not yet a defined bundle, integration, pricing model, or customer segment.

4. **High-value employment / consulting path**
   - Seek a roughly $500k/year role doing this kind of systems/product work for a well-capitalized organization.
   - This is a career-option hypothesis, separate from deciding to commercialize TGW assets directly.

5. **Catio layer**
   - Consider whether the Catio state-machine/gated-workflow layer can be offered as a product.
   - Any future product definition must distinguish the generalizable enforcement/runtime layer from TGW-specific workflows, data, evidence, and operator practices.
