# Addendum: the existing camera tool is also proof of concept

**State:** `capture-staged` — Dave’s framing; no extraction, productization, or release authorized
**Date:** 2026-07-20

The current TGW camera/collection implementation is also a proof of concept, not a blank-slate app concept.

Dave states it has a simple behavior no other camera app provides. That behavior—not a generalized claim that TGW has a better camera app—is the first thing to inspect and understand in its actual collection workflow.

The future examination must begin with demonstration and evidence:

1. Observe the exact behavior in the existing tool.
2. Identify the operator problem it removes and why usual camera apps fail it.
3. Separate implementation-specific details and TGW-private workflow/data from the general interaction pattern.
4. Determine whether it is an internal advantage only, an internal improvement opportunity, or a safely generalizable product wedge.

The value of the work does not depend on external commercialization; it can improve the existing tool even if the behavior remains TGW-specific.
