Thinking Mode
The model generates a hidden (or visible) chain-of-thought to break down a problem before providing the final answer.

    When to use: Use this for complex debugging, multi-step math, logical puzzles, semantic classification across large datasets, and advanced reasoning. It helps prevent hallucinations and ensures accuracy on tricky queries, though it takes longer and uses more tokens.
    Control Parameter: {"thinking": {"type": "enabled"}} combined with a reasoning_effort of high (default) or max.
